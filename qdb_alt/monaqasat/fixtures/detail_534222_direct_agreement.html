Companies wishing to participate in government tenders must be classified. Please note that linking tender financial ceilings to company financial limits for suppliers and service providers has been postponed to allow more companies to meet the advanced classification requirements. Updates will be announced on the website - The Ministry of Finance announces the postponement of the mandatory submission of the ICV certificate as a condition for participating in government tenders until the beginning of 2027. You will be notified of the specific date. However, The Ministry of Finance encourages you to initiate the process to obtain ICV Certificate, and you can access the required information through the Monaqasat Portal
